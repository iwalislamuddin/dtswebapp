tags: [new, improved]

- **37 calculators to Indonesian SNI codes:** Concrete, Steel, Substructure, Geotechnical, Loading, Hydraulics and
  Structural Analysis, with a rewritten calculation engine tested against the previous version.
- **New interface:** a desktop-style layout with File & Help menus, navigation by category, Input and Detail tabs,
  light & dark themes, and a new logo and icons. On phones, number fields have −/+ buttons and common-value chips.
- **Edifisia account:** sign in with email or Google — one account for every Edifisia app.
- **Save & Open Work (Premium):** save inputs to your Edifisia account (cloud, up to 50 jobs, available on any device)
  or as an .edft file.
- **Reports with formulas:** the Detail tab shows the full calculation steps; illustrated PDF reports (Premium) are
  available in Indonesian and English.
- **O'Neill & Reese pile capacity:** Pile Capacity (SPT) now uses this FHWA method for drilled shafts, replacing the
  Meyerhof SPT method. Clay: side resistance α·cu and end bearing Nc*·cu up to 3,830 kPa. Sand: side resistance β·σ′v
  up to 200 kPa with β from depth and SPT N, end bearing 57.5N up to 4,300 kPa. An option ignores clay side resistance
  over the top 1.5 m and the bottom 1D, and the cu you enter is now used. The tool also covers an SPT N table, pile
  cut-off, negative skin friction, uplift capacity and pile reinforcement.
- **New tools:** Seismic Load (SNI 1726:2019) from the Indonesian seismic hazard maps, with design spectrum, seismic
  design category and base shear; Concrete Beam Design Check (flexure, shear, torsion).
- **Tool improvements:** pinned & fixed Base Plate; P–M Column Diagram for multiple load combinations; Pile Cap with
  free pile coordinates and a reinforcement sketch; Wind Load for gable warehouses, free-standing roofs and open
  frames.
- **Example links:** examples from the Try page on edifisia.com open in Civil Tools with the inputs already filled in.

Saved work that used the old SPT method opens with the O'Neill & Reese method, so results may differ from before.
Please check them again before use.
